Hackathon project · StartupTN problem statement

Most students pick a career on a coin flip. We built the compass.

CareerCompass AI is an AI-powered career development ecosystem that walks a student from career discovery to skill-building and employment readiness — not a quiz that spits out a job title and disappears.

Try the career interview → See how it works

"Every student deserves a compass, not a coin flip."

01 — the problem

Students from Tamil-medium and rural schools pick a branch, then hope for the best.

Manystudents in Tamil Nadu pick a branch with no real guidance
First-Gencollege students often have no family career network
4 Yearscan be spent in the wrong stream before it's noticed

Students from Tamil-medium and rural schools often pick engineering branches with no counselor and no roadmap to guide the choice. Getting it wrong means years spent in a mismatched stream, graduating with skills that don't line up with the job market.

WhatsApp groups and YouTube videos offer generic advice — not guidance personalised to a student's actual skills and situation. First-generation college students have no family network to lean on. CareerCompass AI aims to be that first reliable guide.

02 — the idea

Existing platforms recommend careers. CareerCompass develops them.

It's not just a career-recommendation platform — it's an AI-powered ecosystem that guides a student from discovery to skill development, preparation, and employment readiness.

Personalised

Matches each student's unique mix of skills, subjects, hobbies, and academic background — not a generic quiz.

AI-powered

Uses content-based filtering and cosine similarity to compare a student's profile against a career dataset.

Actionable

Delivers a step-by-step learning roadmap and skill-gap analysis — not just a career label.

05 — how it works

From interview to roadmap.

first

Login & Gemini interview

  • Gemini asks questions dynamically
  • Follow-ups based on prior answers
  • No predefined questionnaire
  • Ends when enough is known
then

Profile extraction & matching

  • Extracts skills, interests, subjects
  • Matches profile to career dataset
  • Ranks options by similarity score
  • Built with Gemini & scikit-learn
finally

Output to student

  • Top 3 recommended careers
  • Why each career fits
  • Skill gap — what's missing
  • Learning roadmap — next steps

What the interview actually feels like

Gemini asks one question at a time and decides what to ask next based on the answer — no multiple choice, just the student's own words.

Reliability — "what if a student lies?" Gemini cross-checks consistency through follow-up questions. Phase 2 adds validation against aptitude tests, resumes, and academic records.

Algorithm design

Every recommendation is backed by a specific, named technique — not a black box.

Content-based filtering
Matches careers based on similarity between the student's profile and career profiles.
Cosine similarity
Measures the mathematical distance between current skills and the skills a career requires — used for skill-gap analysis.
Weighted scoring model
Weighs skills, interests, and academic performance to produce an ordered shortlist of careers.
Random Forest (planned)
Once real student outcome data is collected, this model will learn from successful matches.

Where career data comes from

Gemini doesn't invent careers — it works from a structured dataset built by the team, informed by NCS categories, O*NET-style occupation groupings, and AICTE skill framework references. This is a self-curated dataset, not a live API connection yet — Gemini can only select from and reason over what's already defined here. Phase 2 connects live to NCS & O*NET APIs.

Responsible AI

Career guidance touches a student's future — the team takes that seriously.

Secure by design
Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and a per-user salt before they reach the database — the plain text is never stored. Sessions use signed tokens.
Suggestions, not decisions
The student always makes the final call.
Consent first (Phase 2)
Clear opt-in before the AI interview begins.
Human review
A teacher sees the shape of their class; a parent sees one student’s result, and only after that student shares a code. Neither sees the transcript.
No data sold
Student data stays within CareerCompass — never shared with third parties.
12 — MVP vs. what's next

The road ahead

Phase 1 — MVP (built)

  • Gemini career interview — open questions, no fixed script
  • Recommendation engine over 26 careers (scikit-learn cosine similarity)
  • Weighted scoring: 70% interview profile, 30% academic marks
  • Skill-gap analysis and a learning roadmap
  • Accounts for students, teachers and parents
  • Consistency check — Gemini asks for a real example and flags claims nothing backs up
  • Teacher dashboard: class distribution, common gaps, workshop ideas
  • Parent dashboard: matches, progress over time, how to help at home
  • Full Tamil interface and Tamil interview
  • Installable on a phone home screen, with a QR code for classrooms
  • Post-interview feedback: usefulness, confidence before and after
  • Flask + SQLite backend, ready to deploy on Render

Phase 2 — enhancements

  • Live NCS & O*NET API integration
  • Consent flow & human counselor review
  • Resume analyser — auto-extract skills
  • More languages — Hindi, Telugu and beyond
  • Resume, internship & scholarship matching
  • Voice-based career counselor
